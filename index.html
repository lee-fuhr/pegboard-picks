How we choose our picks

We never pretend to run our own timed tests or abuse pegboards in a lab. Instead, we rely on what actually matters: deep dives into published reviews from woodworking and garage-organisation experts, years of owner feedback on forums and retailer pages, and common failure patterns we’ve observed across brands. A pegboard that warps, snaps hooks, or strips its mounting holes after a year of typical use doesn’t make our list, no matter how pretty it looks. We also check long-term availability of accessories and replacement parts, nothing is more frustrating than a great board with discontinued hooks. Our picks are systems that have maintained strong reputations over time, with honest trade-offs clearly noted. If a product is known for bent hooks or flimsy bracket clips, we say so.
