A good pegboard turns a cluttered wall into a clean, visual organization system for tools, craft supplies, or kitchen gear. Unlike cabinets or drawers, pegboards keep everything out in the open; you can grab a screwdriver or a whisk at a glance and put it back just as fast. The key is choosing the right material and hole pattern for your needs. Budget-conscious shoppers often start with hardboard (Masonite) panels, which are lightweight and cheap but sag under heavy loads. For a long‑term investment, look for steel or heavy‑duty fiberboard with a 1-inch hole pattern and a weight rating that matches what you plan to hang.
